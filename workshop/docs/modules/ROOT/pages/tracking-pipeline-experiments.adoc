= Organize and track pipelines with experiments

[role="_abstract"]
Now that you've created and run a pipeline, use an experiment to track and organize your pipeline runs.

OpenShift AI utilizes MLflow for pipeline experiment tracking. An experiment serves as the primary organizational container used to group, manage, and compare related machine learning runs for a specific project or business objective.

.Procedure
1. Refer to xref:running-your-pipeline.adoc[Run your pipeline] to create a second pipeline run. This time, under *MLflow Integration*, enable MLflow Experiment Tracking and specify an experiment name.
+
image::pipelines/mlflow-create-experiment.png[Create an MLflow experiment]

2. View your newly-created experiment in the OpenShift AI Console under the Experiments tab:
+
image::pipelines/pipelines-experiments.png[OpenShift AI Pipelines Experiments]

3. Click your experiment name, and you'll see your latest pipeline run:
+
image::pipelines/pipelines-experiments-console.png[OpenShift AI Pipelines Experiments Console]

4. Click your pipeline run, and you'll get an overview of pipeline input parameters. Scalar metric artifacts output by the run will appear under the *Model Metrics* heading.
