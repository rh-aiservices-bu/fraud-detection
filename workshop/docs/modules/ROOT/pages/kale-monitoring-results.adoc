= Monitoring results and metrics

[role="_abstract"]
After running the Kale pipeline, you can monitor its progress and review the results in the {productname-short} Pipelines UI.

.Prerequisites

* You compiled and submitted the pipeline as described in xref:kale-compiling-and-running.adoc[Compiling and running the pipeline].

.Procedure

. In the {productname-short} dashboard, navigate to your project and click the *Pipelines* tab.

. Click on the `fraud-detection-kale` pipeline and then click *View runs*.

. Click on your run to view the pipeline execution progress. Each step shows its status (pending, running, succeeded, or failed).

. Click on individual steps to view their logs. For example:
* *get_data* — shows the dataset download progress and row counts.
* *train* — shows the Keras training output with loss and accuracy per epoch.
* *evaluate* — shows the accuracy, precision, and recall metrics.

. Review the pipeline metrics. The `pipeline-metrics` cell prints accuracy, precision, and recall values. These metrics appear in the KFP run details:
+
* *Accuracy* — overall prediction accuracy on the test set
* *Precision* — proportion of predicted frauds that are actual frauds
* *Recall* — proportion of actual frauds that are detected

. Verify the model upload. In your S3 storage, confirm that `models/fraud/1/model.onnx` exists.

.Verification

* All 5 pipeline steps show a succeeded status.
* The pipeline metrics (accuracy, precision, recall) are visible in the run details.
* The `models/fraud/1/model.onnx` file is in your S3 bucket.

.Next step

* Distribute model training, as described in xref:distributing-training-jobs.adoc[Distributing training jobs].
